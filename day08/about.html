OUR STORY

Make space for meaningful work.

TaskFlow is a fictional software product concept that explores how teams can plan clearly, collaborate thoughtfully, and grow together.

WHY WE EXIST

Less noise.
More direction.

We imagined TaskFlow as a calmer alternative to scattered project updates, unclear tasks, and last-minute surprises. This learning project brings that idea to life with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and lightweight JavaScript.
